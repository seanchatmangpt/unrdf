# Chapter 8: Conclusion and Future Work

The UNRDF project definitively proves that knowledge graphs can transcend their passive origins. By marrying the formalisms of Object-Centric Process Mining, CRDT-backed temporal event streams, and nanosecond-scale WebAssembly engineering, we have delivered the foundations of Vision 2030. Future work will investigate scaling vector causality across planetary-scale federations and natively integrating fully autonomous LLMs via the Model Context Protocol (MCP) to drive ontology generation dynamically.
